Last updated: 30 October 2026

How to delete what Rewordium – Smart Keyboard keeps about you.

01On your phone

  • Clear learned words in the app’s Keys tab, clear Jade chats in Settings, and clear clipboard history from the keyboard’s clipboard.
  • Or remove everything at once: Android Settings › Apps › Rewordium › Storage › Clear storage, or uninstall the app.

02If you signed in

Open the app › Settings › Delete account. This deletes your profile (including your included AI uses) and your sign-in account. Unshare any themes you published first if you want them gone too.

03If you never signed in, or can’t open the app

Email noxquilltech@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my data”. Tell us the email you signed in with, or, if you never signed in, the approximate date you installed the app and your phone model so we can find the anonymous profile. We’ll delete it and confirm by email.

04What we delete

  • Your app profile: included AI uses, sign-in method, push token, and name and email if you signed in.
  • Your install records.
  • Themes you published, if you ask.

Analytics data is kept in aggregated form by Google Analytics for Firebase according to our project’s retention setting.
