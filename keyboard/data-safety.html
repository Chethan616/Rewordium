02Never collected

  • What you type with the keyboard (keystrokes, words, messages), except text you choose to send to an AI tool.
  • Words the keyboard learns, your clipboard history, contacts and photos. These stay on your phone.
  • The advertising ID. The app has no ads.
  • Precise location.

03Security and control

  • Data sent off your phone is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).
  • You can delete your data: see “Delete your data”.
  • AI tools, the accessibility assistant, contact suggestions and notifications are all optional.
