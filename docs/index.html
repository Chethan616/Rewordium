Your Private AI Writing Partner

Rewordium is a premium, privacy-first AI writing assistant and custom keyboard designed specifically for Android. It lets you refine your emails, change writing tones, instantly check grammar, translate, and chat with AI directly inside any app you are currently typing in.

By blending a beautiful, high-performance custom keyboard with a smart floating shortcut bubble, Rewordium integrates writing intelligence directly into apps like WhatsApp, Gmail, Twitter/X, Slack, and Notion—without forcing you to copy and paste text back and forth.

The Floating AI Assistant Bubble

If you prefer using your device's native keyboard (like Gboard or Samsung Keyboard), Rewordium provides a beautiful, floating AI Assistant Bubble. This bubble hovers unobtrusively on the edge of your screen when an active input field is focused.

How it works safely: Tapping the floating bubble tells Rewordium to temporarily inspect the text inside your current text box, refine it according to the AI tool you select, and paste the results back instantly. No manual copy-pasting required!

Cross-Application Compatibility

Because it operates through Android's standardized framework, the Assistant Bubble works inside practically any application:

  • Messaging: WhatsApp, Telegram, Signal, Discord, Facebook Messenger.
  • Email: Gmail, Outlook, Yahoo Mail, ProtonMail.
  • Socials: Twitter/X, Instagram, LinkedIn, Threads.
  • Productivity: Slack, Notion, Google Docs, Keep, Evernote.

Context-Aware suggestions: If you tap the Assistant Bubble when your text input field is completely empty, the system safely scans the surrounding page layout. It recognizes the context (e.g., replying to a specific email subject or filling a chat dialog) and automatically suggests contextually relevant replies to kickstart your draft.

Our Absolute Data Commitment

Writing is personal. What you type represents your private chats, work secrets, financial plans, and personal thoughts. Rewordium is built on the absolute rule of **Zero-Data-Collection**. We do not mine your text, sell your logs, or build advertising profiles on you.

Local Secure Storage

AES-256 Key Encryption

Your private API keys, custom writer personas, and personal settings are saved strictly on your local device. We use hardware-backed local keystores (Android Keystore / iOS Keychain) with AES-256 encryption. Your keys never leave your phone.

Keylogger-Free Code

No Typing Loggers

Rewordium's custom keyboard does not log your keystrokes, passwords, or personal details. The typing loop runs entirely local in offline memory, safely generating word suggestions without background data-sharing.

Data Sovereignty

Qwen via Groq (No Chinese Endpoints)

Although Rewordium leverages the advanced open-source Qwen model, your drafts never touch Chinese networks. All requests route to Groq's secure Western servers (USA) where text is processed stateless in-transit and instantly forgotten upon completion.

Anti-Spyware Tamper Safeguards

To guarantee your safety against modified versions, Rewordium integrates strict installation integrity detectors. If the app detects it has been tampered with or modified (as is common with cracked applications that could inject keyloggers), it immediately stops running and locks interaction to keep your data secure.

Step-by-Step Setup & FAQs

Getting started with Rewordium is simple. Follow these straightforward guidelines to activate your keyboard and floating bubble, and read our clear answers to frequent customer questions.

Step 1

Enable the Keyboard

Open your phone's Settings app. Go to System ➔ Languages & input ➔ Virtual keyboard ➔ Manage keyboards, and turn on the Rewordium Keyboard toggle.

Step 2

Set as Default

Focus any text box on your screen. Swipe down on your notification panel, tap Choose input method (or tap the keyboard icon at the bottom right), and select Rewordium.

Step 3

Enable Floating Bubble

Open Rewordium's app. Tap Enable Accessibility Service. In settings, go to Installed Services, choose Rewordium, and toggle it to active. The bubble is now ready!

Frequently Asked Questions

❓ Privacy, Security, & System Warnings

Why does Android show a scary warning when I enable the keyboard? Android shows a standard system prompt for every single third-party keyboard you install, warning that it might collect keystrokes or credit cards. Rewordium is fully audited, keylogger-free, and automatically disables all AI integrations and suggestions when you focus a system password input field.
Why does the Assistant Bubble require Accessibility permissions? Android requires the Accessibility permission so the floating bubble can read the text block you are editing and replace it with your newly selected AI tone. We only read the focused input box, and never look at background activities, personal photos, or app lists.
Are my private API keys uploaded to your cloud servers? No. Your API keys are saved strictly on your local device. They are encrypted using the device's hardware security modules (Android Keystore / iOS Keychain) and sent directly to Google, OpenAI, or Anthropic during AI calls.
Since you use the Qwen AI model, does my text go to servers in China? Absolutely not. While Qwen is a world-class open-source language model developed by Alibaba, Rewordium runs it entirely outside of Chinese networks. Every single request is processed securely through Groq's high-speed US-based servers. Your writing stays fully within secure Western data protection frameworks and never contacts Chinese API endpoints.

❓ Credits, Billing, & Features

How does the credits system work? Each text refinement call (e.g., Tone Shift or Summarizer) through our default cloud server costs exactly 1 credit. Free tiers receive daily automatic credit refills. You can purchase upgrades or configure BYOK for unlimited free usage.
What happens when I enable Bring Your Own Key (BYOK)? Enabling BYOK activates credit-exempt mode. You are no longer charged any credits inside the app! Generations route directly using your private developer keys, billed strictly to your provider account with no markups.
Does the app support local document files? Yes! The Summarizer tool supports local file uploads (PDFs, TXT, DOCX files). Your files are processed locally on the device and chunked into summary segments securely.
