🚨 MANDATORY ANDROID ACCESSIBILITY SERVICE DISCLOSURE

Rewordium integrates Android’s standard AccessibilityService API to provide its core real-time AI writing companion features. This integration is optional but critical for the floating AI Assistant Bubble to function.

1. Scope and Purpose of Accessibility Service

  • Inline Text Reading: When you actively focus a text input field in a third-party application and tap the floating Rewordium Assistant Bubble, the service safely reads the active draft inside that input field.
  • Context-Aware Replied Suggestion: If you tap the Assistant Bubble when your active text input box is completely blank, the service contextually scans surrounding visible page elements (such as an incoming email subject or previous chat line) strictly to draft a relevant response.
  • Direct Inline Text Replacement: After our secure AI completion completes, the service automatically replaces your active draft in the text field with the refined, polished version. This eliminates manual copy-pasting.

2. Operational Limitations & Boundaries

  • Automatic Password Suspension: The Accessibility Service automatically suspends all monitoring, reading, and AI suggest layers whenever a secure password, credit card, or pin entry input field is active on your device.
  • No Background Surveillance: The Accessibility Service does not run passive background tracking. It does not monitor apps, catalog browsing history, inspect personal photo files, capture device screens, or record calls.
  • Opt-In Control: Enabling the Accessibility Service is 100% voluntary. You must explicitly authorize it in Android Settings (Settings > Accessibility > Installed Services > Rewordium). You can revoke this permission at any moment without deleting your account.

🔒 1. Zero-Data-Collection & Privacy-First Architecture

Rewordium is engineered on a strict privacy-by-design framework. Our business model does not rely on gathering, profiling, mining, or selling user typing content, communications, or personal metadata.

  • No Telemetry Keylogging: Our keyboard input engine runs completely locally in offline RAM. We do not maintain any logs of your keystrokes, personal chats, custom dictionaries, or search queries.
  • Smart Clipboard Privacy: The clipboard recommendation panel processes copied text segments strictly locally. It automatically ignores and discards passwords, security pins, credit card details, and raw phone numbers from showing up as suggestions.

📊 2. Information We Collect and Process

To run account-based billing, standard credit daily allocations, and active security systems, we process the following categories of data:

2.1 Account and Security Information

Email address, secure Google Sign-In or Firebase tokens, and personal app preferences (e.g. customized user personas, saved settings, and interface themes).

2.2 Billing & Subscription Data

Subscription status records, transaction IDs, and credit balances. All payment information is securely processed and stored exclusively by Google Play Billing; Rewordium never collects or views credit card details.

2.3 Transient Text Processing Data (Stateless Cloud Transit)

Text submitted for default cloud AI edits (Tone Shift, Summarizer, Translator, Grammar Check) is securely transited over HTTPS, processed transiently in secure RAM, and immediately deleted upon completion. Text is never logged to disk or used for AI retraining.

2.4 Secure Local Storage (Hardware-Backed Encryption)

Your private API keys (OpenAI, Gemini, Claude) are stored strictly locally on your device's hardware partition using 256-bit AES cryptographic encryption (Android Keystore / iOS Keychain) via FlutterSecureStorage. These keys never touch our servers.

🌐 3. Data Sovereignty & AI Infrastructure (Qwen via Groq)

We have established rigid infrastructural isolation boundaries to protect your writing from international telemetry tracking:

  • Default AI Engine: Rewordium leverages the advanced open-source Qwen 3 (32B) model.
  • Groq US Infrastructure: 100% of these default model completions route directly through Groq's secure, US-based server networks.
  • Zero Chinese Endpoints: Writing requests are completely isolated from Chinese domestic endpoints, Alibaba Cloud servers, or foreign-governed networks. Your drafts are kept entirely within secure US-based infrastructure.

🔑 4. Bring Your Own Key (BYOK) Data Processing

When utilizing your own custom API keys:

  • Direct Secure Dispatch: Keys are fetched locally from your secure Android Keystore and transmitted directly over secure HTTPS tunnels to the provider's endpoints (e.g. OpenAI, Google Gemini, Anthropic Claude).
  • Complete Server Bypass: Your keys and text drafts completely bypass Rewordium's servers in BYOK mode.
  • Terms & Privacy: All BYOK actions fall under the respective provider's terms of service and privacy agreements.

🛡️ 5. Tampering, Safety, & APK Integrity Safeguards

To protect users from tampered copies of Rewordium (which may have spyware or keyloggers repackaged by bad actors):

  • Signature Locks: Rewordium integrates signature integrity checks that cross-reference installer signatures against official certificates.
  • Automated Lockout: If a modified, cracked, or tampered APK is detected, the Service immediately stops running and locks functions to secure your keystream.
  • Telemetry Disclaimer: Modified copies invalidate all privacy and data sovereignty guarantees. Always install strictly from the official Google Play Store.

📝 6. Open Source Software Components

The keyboard component is based on FlorisBoard, an open-source Android keyboard developed by Patrick Goldinger and contributors, licensed under the Apache License 2.0. Open-source keyboard components operate strictly offline in local memory, processing data entirely on-device.

🌎 7. GDPR & CCPA Compliance

GDPR (European Union): The lawful basis for processing transient text is contract performance. EU users enjoy portability, erasure, and objection rights, alongside access to our Data Protection Officer.

CCPA (California): California residents hold a strict "Do Not Sell My Info" guarantee. Rewordium sells zero user data or typing history.
