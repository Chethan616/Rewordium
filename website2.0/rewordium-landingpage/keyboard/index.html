Rewordium – Smart Keyboard

A smart Android keyboard with intelligent autocorrect, swipe typing and suggestions, plus optional AI writing tools. Your typing stays on your phone.

01A keyboard first

  • Intelligent autocorrect that leaves names and slang alone.
  • Accurate swipe (glide) typing.
  • Smart word suggestions and next-word predictions.
  • A personal dictionary that learns your words, on your phone. Incognito mode when you want it to forget.
  • Clipboard, emoji search, emoticons, GIFs and stickers, including stickers you make or import.
  • Themes: ready-made packs, a theme builder, and a community gallery.

02Optional AI writing tools

Rewrite, change the tone, summarize, translate or tidy up grammar right from the keyboard, or chat with Jade, the in-app writing assistant.

  • The app includes a limited number of AI uses, shown in the app. They don’t refill in this version, and AI is never unlimited.
  • When they’re used up, the keyboard keeps working exactly the same.
  • Prefer your own AI provider? Add your own key (Groq, OpenAI, Google Gemini or Anthropic).
  • Text is sent to the AI provider only when you use a tool.

05FAQ

Do I need an account?

No. Everything works without signing in. Signing in is optional.

Is what I type sent anywhere?

No. Typing, autocorrect and suggestions run on your phone. Text is only sent when you use an AI tool, and then only the text you chose goes to the AI provider.

Is AI unlimited?

No. The app includes a limited number of AI uses, shown in the app, and they don’t refill in this version. You can use your own AI key instead.

What happens when my AI uses run out?

Nothing else changes: the keyboard, themes, stickers, GIFs and every non-AI feature keep working.

Which AI provider is used?

The built-in AI uses models run by Groq. With your own key, your requests go to the provider you choose.

How do I delete my data?

See “Delete your data”.
