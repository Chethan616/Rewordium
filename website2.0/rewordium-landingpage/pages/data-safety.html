Data Safety

App: Rewordium · Contact: noxquilltech@gmail.com

A plain-English summary of what we collect, how it's used, and who it's shared with. Short version: very little, only to make the app work, and never for ads.

01Data Collection

Rewordium may collect limited user data necessary to provide the app's core functionality:

Personal Information (if applicable)

  • Email address (used for login or support communication)

Usage Data

  • Input text submitted for rewording
  • Interaction logs and usage statistics (for feature improvement)

Device Information

  • Device type, OS version, and crash logs (for stability and diagnostics)

02Data Usage

All data is used strictly for:

  • Processing your rewording requests via an AI model
  • Improving app features and performance
  • Providing user support

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or unrelated analytics.

03Data Sharing

Rewordium shares data only with Groq, our trusted AI inference provider, for real-time language processing.

  • Data shared with Groq is used only to process your request
  • Groq does not store, log, or reuse the data
  • Groq does not share your data with any third parties

No other external services receive your personal or usage data.

04Data Security

We employ standard security practices to safeguard your data:

  • Encrypted communication using HTTPS
  • Internal measures to prevent unauthorised access

05User Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request deletion of your data
  • Request a copy of the data we have on you
  • Contact us regarding any privacy concerns at noxquilltech@gmail.com

We respond to all privacy-related queries promptly.

06Children's Privacy

Rewordium is not designed for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect or process data from children without parental consent.

Little collected. Never sold. Never used for ads.
